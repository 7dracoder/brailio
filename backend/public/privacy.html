Brailio prototype privacy

Brailio stores imported documents, notes, bookmarks, and named room maps on your device. Room scan snapshots are temporary; no room movie is saved. Clear data in Settings removes the local library and room maps.

Apple handles optional location-based place search, Apple walking routes, and speech recognition under its policies. Choosing Google routing sends your current coordinates and selected destination to Brailio's backend and Google Routes. Google routing requires server setup. Opening Google Maps shares the selected destination with Google Maps and pauses Brailio guidance. The Brailio backend does not intentionally log route coordinates or save route responses. Hosting and service providers may process request metadata.

Private demo mode stores a Baseten key only in this iPhone's Keychain and sends selected text or scan snapshots directly to Baseten over HTTPS. The key is not bundled in source or the app binary. Optional server mode instead sends selected content to Brailio's backend and Baseten, with provider credentials on the server. Local handwriting, OCR, and document reading do not require this AI service.

Online document search sends your search text to Internet Archive; downloads contact the selected document host. Only files you select/import enter Brailio's library. This is a development prototype and has not been validated as an independent mobility aid.
