Brailio prototype terms

Brailio is an experimental tactile communication and navigation prototype. Route alignment uses approximate GPS and travel direction; silence does not establish safe surroundings, sidewalk access, or a safe road crossing. Saved room positions can drift and cannot follow moving objects. Continue using your usual mobility aid and verify destinations yourself.

Only download documents you are entitled to access. OCR, handwriting, and AI results may be incorrect. Vibration playback uses a limited English teaching code, not a complete Braille transcription standard.

Google Maps features are also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Apple, Baseten, Internet Archive, and document hosts have their own terms and policies.
